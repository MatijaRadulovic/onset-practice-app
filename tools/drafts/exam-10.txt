== Coral Reefs

== Bridges
Bridges are among the oldest and most important structures that humans have built. The first ones were probably tree trunks laid across a stream, but the Romans already built huge stone arches that have survived for two thousand years. Modern engineers use steel and concrete to span rivers, valleys and even stretches of open sea. A suspension bridge hangs from thick cables and can reach a length of several {miles|hundred metres}, whereas a simple beam bridge is suitable for short distances. Besides their practical use, many bridges have become symbols of their cities.

== Skiing

Archaeologists have found wooden skis that are {thousands of years old|many thousands of years old}, and ancient pictures show hunters gliding through forests. The sport as we know it developed in Norway in the nineteenth century, and it soon became {fashionable|popular} in the Alps. Modern equipment is lighter and safer than before, but accidents still happen, mostly because people {overestimate|overrate} their abilities. Warmer winters have become a serious problem for ski resorts.

== Dreams
Dreams have puzzled people since the beginning of history, and nobody has found a final explanation for them. Most dreams take place during a phase of sleep in which the brain is very active and the eyes move {rapidly|fast} under the closed lids. Some scientists believe that dreaming helps us to process the experiences of the day and to store them in our memory. Others think that the images are merely random signals without any meaning. Whatever the truth is, most people forget the majority of their dreams within a few minutes.
